Cookie Policy

This website uses cookies to ensure you get the best experience. Below you will find a full declaration of the cookies used on eadigital.se, including their purpose and duration. You can update your consent preferences at any time.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and understand how you use it.

How we use cookies

EA Digital uses cookies solely for analytics purposes — to understand how visitors find and use the site so we can improve it. We do not use cookies for advertising or to sell your data.

Your choices

When you first visit eadigital.se, you will be asked to consent to the use of cookies. You can withdraw or change your consent at any time by clicking the cookie icon at the bottom of the page.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some websites.
